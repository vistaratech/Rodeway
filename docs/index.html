🔒 Privacy Policy

Privacy by Design: Rodeway is completely account-free. We do not require registration or personal logins. Camera frames and location data are processed in real time on-device in volatile memory and are never recorded, captured, or transmitted to any external server.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

  • Location Data (Fine, Coarse & Background Location): Used exclusively to calculate turn-by-turn driving routes, project AR waypoints, and determine the precise distance to upcoming intersections to trigger vehicle turn indicators. Real-time coordinates are processed securely with the Google Navigation SDK. We do not store travel histories on remote servers.
  • Camera Permission: Required strictly to display Augmented Reality (AR) visual driving paths over the live camera view. Camera frames are processed locally in real time for AR tracking and lane detection. Video feeds are never recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices: Used solely to discover and communicate with your vehicle's physical ESP32 Smart Indicator hardware module via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to trigger turn signals. No personal information is transmitted over Bluetooth.

2. Third-Party Services

Rodeway integrates trusted third-party SDKs necessary for driving navigation:

  • Google Maps Platform & Google Navigation SDK: Used for map rendering, search autocomplete, and route calculation. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

No Remote User Accounts: Because Rodeway does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles, no personal data is stored on remote databases. Consequently, you do not need to submit an account deletion request.

Local Data Deletion: Any preferences (such as accepted terms) are stored purely on your local Android device via standard local storage. You can instantly and permanently erase all local data at any time by:

  • Going to Android Settings ➡️ Apps ➡️ Rodeway ➡️ Storage & cache ➡️ Clear storage.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

For any data deletion inquiries or questions, contact us directly at info.vistaratech@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

Rodeway is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
