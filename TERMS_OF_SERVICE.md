## 1. Description of Service

Rodeway is a mobile driving assistance application integrating:
- Augmented Reality (AR) route projection via camera feeds.
- Computer vision-assisted lane detection.
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) turn signal trigger integration with ESP32 microcontroller hardware.
- Turn-by-turn navigation using the Google Maps and Google Navigation SDKs.
